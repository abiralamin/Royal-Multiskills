Course Eligibility Requirements

Understanding the eligibility criteria for different certification programs

Core Trade Registration Eligibility

Basic Requirements

  • Minimum 2 years of relevant work experience in construction
  • Basic understanding of construction safety practices
  • Physical fitness to perform construction tasks
  • Valid work permit or employment pass (for foreign workers)
  • Basic literacy and numeracy skills

Trade-Specific Experience

  • Demonstrated experience in the specific trade (carpentry, plumbing, electrical, etc.)
  • Knowledge of tools and equipment relevant to the trade
  • Understanding of trade-specific safety requirements
  • Portfolio or evidence of previous work (where applicable)

Multi-skill Registration Eligibility

Path 1: Two Skills Certificates

  • Hold two (2) recognised Skills Evaluation Certificates [SEC/SEC(K)] of different trades
  • Minimum four (4) years of construction experience in Singapore
  • Certificates must be from different trade categories
  • Valid and current certifications

Path 2: One Certificate + Safety Training

  • One (1) Skills Evaluation Certificate [SEC/SEC(K)]
  • Minimum six (6) years of construction experience in Singapore
  • Completed 120 hours of training in approved safety-related courses
  • Safety training must be from recognized institutions

Path 3: WSQ Advanced Certificate

  • One (1) Skills Evaluation Certificate [SEC/SEC(K)]
  • Minimum six (6) years of construction experience in Singapore
  • Obtained WSQ Advanced Certificate in Workplace Safety and Health
  • Certificate must be current and valid

General Information & Requirements

Documentation Required

  • Valid identification documents
  • Work experience certificates
  • Educational certificates (if applicable)
  • Previous training certificates
  • Medical fitness certificate (for certain courses)

Language Requirements

  • Basic understanding of English
  • Ability to read safety instructions
  • Communication skills for workplace safety
  • Training available in multiple languages

Age & Physical Requirements

  • Minimum age: 18 years
  • Physical fitness for construction work
  • No significant health conditions that may affect safety
  • Ability to work in various weather conditions

Training Commitment

  • Regular attendance required
  • Participation in practical assessments
  • Completion of theoretical examinations
  • Adherence to safety protocols during training
