Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our training programs

What types of training courses do you offer?

We offer comprehensive construction skills training, multi-skill courses, core trade training, R1 & low levy courses, and safety training programs. All courses are designed with proper safety guidelines and modern training facilities.

Do you offer flexible training schedules?

Yes! We're open 7 days a week and offer flexible training periods to accommodate our students' schedules. We understand that many students work and need convenient timing options.

Is installment payment available?

Yes, we offer installment facility to make our training programs more accessible. Please contact us to discuss payment options that work best for you.

Do you provide intensive training for students who need extra help?

Absolutely! We provide intensive training classes for students who need additional support, under experienced trainers' supervision. We're committed to ensuring all students succeed.

How quickly can you arrange urgent exams?

We can arrange urgent exams within the shortest period of time. Our efficient system and high pass rate ensure students can complete their certification quickly when needed.
